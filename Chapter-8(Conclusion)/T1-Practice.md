## 5️⃣ Communication is Very Important

Technical knowledge alone is not enough.

You must: - Speak confidently\
- Explain your project clearly\
- Communicate in simple English

### Practice:

-   Explain your final year project in 2 minutes.\
-   Explain API in simple language.\
-   Explain what happens when user clicks login.
