# Self Introduction -- Seminar

Hi everyone, good morning/afternoon.

My name is Kaleo Garaja, and I have around 11 years of experience in the
IT industry. I started my career as a PHP and web developer, where I
learned and worked with technologies like HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP,
Laravel, and CodeIgniter.

As I progressed, I moved into API development and later expanded my
skills by learning AngularJS, Angular, and the Ionic framework. Using
these technologies, I worked on building web applications and mobile
apps across different domains.

During the early phase of my career, I worked on projects related to
logistics, queue management systems, support ticket management, and job
search management platforms. My first company was Ivanser, a startup and
project-based IT company, where I gained strong hands-on development
experience.

After that, I moved to a product-based company, Mahindra First Choice
Wheels Limited. Working there gave me deeper exposure to product
development. I learned how to manage large volumes of data, maintain
customer history, handle vehicle details and inspection records, support
insurance renewal processes, and work extensively on mobile apps and API
development. This experience helped me understand how real-time products
are designed and maintained.

Later, I joined SBA Global, where I worked on e-book--related projects.
I was involved in digital content optimization, content handling, and
also contributed to building and managing an HR management portal using
Angular.

After that, I moved to Chola (Cholamandalam Investment and Finance
Company), which is an NBFC in the financial sector. Here, I worked on
applications related to different types of loans such as personal loans,
home loans, loans against property, and SME loans. I started with a
support management portal project, then moved into applications based on
the maker--checker concept, where incoming lead data is validated and
processed through multiple levels.

Currently, I am working in the banking domain at ITOTAL Small Finance,
where I am learning more about banking concepts such as savings
accounts, current accounts, and NRI customer management. I am also
handling end-to-end website management by coordinating with vendors and
stakeholders, playing a key role in delivery and execution.

In addition to that, I am managing the complete ownership of the Bharat
Bill Payment System module and related operations.

Overall, my journey has helped me grow from a developer to someone who
understands product development, system design, and delivery
coordination across multiple domains.
